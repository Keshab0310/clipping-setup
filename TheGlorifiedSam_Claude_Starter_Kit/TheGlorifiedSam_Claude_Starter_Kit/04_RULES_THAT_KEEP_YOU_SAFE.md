# Rules that keep you safe

1. **Rights first.** Only clip footage you're allowed to use: your own content, or a campaign that pays you to clip a creator. A public video is not permission.
2. **No botting.** Don't let any tool auto-like, auto-follow or fake engagement. Platforms ban for this fast.
3. **New accounts: go slow.** 1–2 posts a day for the first week. Mass posting from a brand-new account looks like a bot.
4. **Read the campaign page before you clip.** Payout rules, minimum views and required hashtags are all on the page. Don't trust screenshots or Discord rumours.
5. **Never give Claude your passwords.** Sign in yourself; Claude works in your browser after that.
6. **Check everything before it posts.** Have Claude show you the finished clip first, until you trust the routine.
