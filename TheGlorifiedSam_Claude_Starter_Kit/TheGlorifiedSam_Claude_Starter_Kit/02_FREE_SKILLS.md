# Free skills I actually use

Skills live in `~/.claude/skills/`. Claude picks the right one automatically, or you can call it by name.

## Motion graphics pack (MIT licence), by Barty-Bart
https://github.com/Barty-Bart/motion-graphics
```
npx skills add Barty-Bart/motion-graphics
```
- **motion-broll**: B-roll graphics timed to your words
- **sound-effects**: real sound design on the beat, mastered properly (no cheap beeps)
- **object-separation**: cut yourself or a product out of a video, locally
- **explainer-high-energy / default / engaging**: hype promos and product explainers

## Bang Motion (MIT licence), by Bang Tutorial
https://github.com/bangtutorial/bang-motion
Cinematic openers, kinetic typography and explainers built in HTML and rendered to MP4.
Install: clone the repo and copy the folder into `~/.claude/skills/bang-motion`. Skip any paid bridges it mentions.

## Free tools worth knowing
- **LosslessCut** (free app): trim long recordings instantly with no re-encode. https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut
- **gl-transitions** (MIT): 200+ GPU transitions Claude can use in renders. https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions

## How to use a skill
Just ask: *"Use the sound-effects skill on this video"* or *"Make a 15-second hype promo for my Discord."*
