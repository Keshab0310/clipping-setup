# Free resources (and what their licences allow)

Always read the licence page yourself. Licences change.

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Music | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/ | Free for YouTube and social. Not for TV, CDs or games. |
| Music | https://pixabay.com/music/ | Free, but some tracks get Content ID claims. Keep your licence proof. |
| Sound effects | https://kenney.nl/assets (CC0) and https://freesound.org (filter to CC0) | CC0 means you can do anything with it |
| Sound effects | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/ | Free for videos |
| Fonts | https://fonts.google.com | Open licence, use anywhere |
| Fonts | https://www.fontshare.com | Free for commercial use. Download them yourself, don't share the files. |
| Icons | https://lucide.dev, https://phosphoricons.com, https://tabler.io/icons | Open source |
| Captions | whisper-cpp (see Setup) | Runs on your computer, free |
