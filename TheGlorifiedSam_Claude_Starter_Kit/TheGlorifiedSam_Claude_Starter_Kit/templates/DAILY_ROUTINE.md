# Daily routine (paste into a scheduled task)

In the Claude app: Code tab > Scheduled > New task. Or just tell Claude: "Schedule this every day at 10 AM."

---
Every morning:
1. Read my CLAUDE.md.
2. Check my last 7 days of posts (views, likes, comments) and tell me what worked and what flopped, in 5 lines.
3. Find 3 trending ideas in my niche from today.
4. Draft 3 hooks for each idea (first line under 8 words, readable with the sound off).
5. If there's new footage in my Raw folder, transcribe it and list the 5 best moments with timestamps.
6. Don't post anything. Put everything in a file called `today.md` and send me a short summary.
