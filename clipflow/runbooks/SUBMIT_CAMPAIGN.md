# Submit a posted clip to a campaign

Many campaigns accept a clip only within 30 minutes of posting. `clipflow.py posted` prints the deadline.

**Before approving a clip for a campaign,** read the live campaign page. Confirm the footage is allowed, plus the pay per 1K views, minimum payout, required hashtags, CTA, overlay, allowed platforms, and deadline. Do not rely on screenshots.

1. Open the exact campaign named on the queue item and click Submit clip.
2. If a Discord popup appears, choose Not now, then Skip for now. Do not join anything.
3. Paste the post link into Clip link and wait for the checks to load.
4. Read the requirements. If the clip fails any of them, stop and report it.
5. **Stop before Submit.** Show the user the campaign, link, and requirements, then wait for a clear yes in chat.
6. After that yes, tick the requirements box, click Submit clip, and wait for "Clip submitted".
7. Record it: `python clipflow.py submitted ID --platform PLATFORM`

New TikToks can show "couldn't read" for 5 to 10 minutes. Wait, then try again before the deadline.
