# Game Reference: House Edge & Strategy

Consult this when giving specific numbers or correct play. House edge = the casino's long-run average take per bet resolved. Lower is better for the player. Figures are typical; exact rules at a given table shift them (always say so when it matters).

## 1. House edge by game (lower = better for player)

| Game | House edge (good rules / best bet) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Blackjack** | ~0.5% | Requires basic strategy AND 3:2 payouts. 6:5 blackjack adds ~1.4% — avoid it. |
| **Video poker** | ~0.46% (9/6 Jacks or Better, perfect play) | Pay table is everything; most machines pay worse than 9/6. |
| **Baccarat (Banker)** | ~1.06% | Best bet at the table. 5% commission already factored in. |
| **Craps (pass/come + odds)** | 1.41% on the line, lower overall with odds | Taking max odds dilutes the edge toward 0 on the odds portion. |
| **Baccarat (Player)** | ~1.24% | Fine second choice. |
| **Pai Gow Poker** | ~1.5% (playing the "house way") | Slow, low-variance, lots of pushes. Banking lowers it further. |
| **Roulette (European, single 0)** | 2.70% | "La partage"/"en prison" on even-money bets cuts it to ~1.35%. |
| **Three Card Poker (Pair Plus)** | ~2.3% | Ante/Play bet ~3.4%. |
| **Casino War** | ~2.9% | Always go to war on ties; surrendering is worse. |
| **Let It Ride** | ~3.5% | |
| **Roulette (American, double 0)** | 5.26% | The extra 00 nearly doubles single-zero edge. Prefer single-zero wheels. |
| **Caribbean Stud** | ~5.2% | |
| **Slots** | ~2%–10%+ (often 5–10%) | Opaque, fast, high hold. Entertainment, not strategy. |
| **Keno** | ~25%–30% | Among the worst bets in the house. |
| **Big Six / Money Wheel** | ~11%–24% | Avoid. |

**One-line summary for a casual player:** best value for table time is **blackjack (3:2, basic strategy)**, **baccarat Banker**, **craps pass-line with odds**, or **single-zero roulette**. Worst value is **keno**, the **money wheel**, and most **slots/side bets**.

## 2. Best and worst bets within key games

**Craps**
- Best: Pass/Come and Don't Pass/Don't Come, then back them with **odds** (the only true-odds, zero-edge bet in the casino). Place 6 and 8 are okay (1.52%).
- Worst: Any-7 (~16.7%), hardways, "Big 6/8", most one-roll proposition bets in the center.

**Baccarat**
- Best: **Banker** (1.06%). Player (1.24%) is fine.
- Worst: **Tie** (~14.4% on 8 decks). Never bet it. Most side bets are bad too.

**Roulette**
- All inside/outside bets on a given wheel carry the *same* house edge — so it's about the *wheel*: single-zero beats double-zero, and French rules (la partage) on even-money bets are best.
- The "0 and 00" five-number bet on American wheels is the one exception and is *worse* (7.89%) — never make it.

**Blackjack**
- Best: just play basic strategy at a 3:2, S17, double-after-split table.
- Worst: **Insurance** / "even money" (a sucker bet unless counting), and any **6:5** table.

**Three Card Poker**: Pair Plus (~2.3%) is the friendlier wager; play the Ante/Play with the standard rule below.
- Ante/Play optimal rule: **raise on Queen-Six-Four or better, fold worse.**

## 3. Blackjack basic strategy (4–8 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed)

Read across to the dealer's up-card. H=hit, S=stand, D=double (hit if not allowed), P=split, Su=surrender (else hit).

**Hard totals (no ace, or ace counted as 1)**
- 8 or less: **H** always
- 9: **D** vs 3–6, else **H**
- 10: **D** vs 2–9, else **H**
- 11: **D** vs 2–10, **H** vs A
- 12: **S** vs 4–6, else **H**
- 13–16: **S** vs 2–6, else **H**
- 17 or more: **S** always

**Soft totals (ace counted as 11)**
- A,2 and A,3: **D** vs 5–6, else **H**
- A,4 and A,5: **D** vs 4–6, else **H**
- A,6: **D** vs 3–6, else **H**
- A,7 (soft 18): **D** vs 3–6; **S** vs 2, 7, 8; **H** vs 9, 10, A
- A,8 (soft 19), A,9 (soft 20): **S** always

**Pairs**
- A,A: **P** always
- 10,10: **S** always (never split a 20)
- 9,9: **P** vs 2–9 except 7; **S** vs 7, 10, A
- 8,8: **P** always
- 7,7: **P** vs 2–7, else **H**
- 6,6: **P** vs 2–6, else **H**
- 5,5: never split — treat as a 10 (**D** vs 2–9, else **H**)
- 4,4: **P** vs 5–6, else **H**
- 3,3 and 2,2: **P** vs 2–7, else **H**

**Surrender (if offered):** 16 vs 9, 10, A → **Su**; 15 vs 10 → **Su**.

**Never take insurance** as a basic-strategy player.

Notes: If the dealer **hits soft 17 (H17)**, a few plays shift (e.g. double 11 vs A, double A,8 vs 6, surrender 15 vs A and 17 vs A) and the house edge rises slightly — mention the table rule matters but the chart above is a near-optimal default.

## 4. Video poker note

Returns depend entirely on the **pay table**, read off the machine. The benchmark is **9/6 Jacks or Better** (pays 9× for a full house, 6× for a flush) → ~99.54% return with perfect strategy. Lower pay tables (8/5, etc.) are meaningfully worse. Full-pay Deuces Wild can theoretically exceed 100% but is rare and demands a different, exact strategy. For casual play: find the best pay table, play max coins (the royal-flush bonus only applies at max bet), and accept that perfect play requires a strategy card.

## 5. Quick framing numbers (for explaining edge to a casual player)

- 1% house edge ≈ losing **$1 per $100 wagered**, on average, over time.
- That's *per bet resolved*, not per trip — a $10 blackjack hand played 60 times/hour wagers $600/hour, so even a 0.5% edge averages ~$3/hour in expected cost (cheap entertainment, if you stick to basic strategy and a budget).
- Slots at 8% over a few hundred fast spins is why bankrolls evaporate quickly there.
- Variance means any single session can swing far from these averages in either direction — which is the fun, and also the trap.
