After clips are compressed and processed, save them on the computer and upload them to Google Drive.

**Why:** The user reviews clips remotely, away from this computer.

**How to apply:** Keep finished clips in a local campaign folder (for the Lacy campaign, `D:\Clipping\lacy_batch\finished`) and upload the same files to a matching private Drive folder. The Drive connector rejects files over about 7.2 MB, so upload a smaller review copy and keep full quality locally. Do not share the folder or post clips without asking.
