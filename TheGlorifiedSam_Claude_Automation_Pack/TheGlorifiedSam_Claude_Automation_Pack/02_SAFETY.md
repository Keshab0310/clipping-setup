# Automate without getting banned

- Only post clips you have the rights to (campaign footage, your own content).
- No auto-likes, auto-follows, auto-comments or view botting. Ever.
- New accounts: 1–2 posts a day for the first week, then build up.
- Check the account before every upload, so you never post to the wrong one.
- Skip clips with slurs, sexual content or anything a campaign's rules ban. Captions burn the words in.
- Read campaign rules on the actual page, not screenshots. Payout rules change.
