# Runbook: submit a clip to a clipping campaign (Content Rewards)

Most campaigns only accept a clip submitted within **30 minutes of posting**, so submit straight after the upload.

1. Open the campaign page and click **Submit clip**.
2. If a "Join … Discord" popup appears, click **Not now**, then **Skip for now**, and open Submit clip again.
3. Paste the post link into **Clip link** and wait about 4 seconds for the checks to load.
4. Tick **"I've read the requirements"** and click **Submit clip**. Wait for **"Clip submitted"**.
5. New TikToks can show "couldn't read" for 5–10 minutes. Wait, then try again.

**Before you clip for any campaign:** read its page and brief. Check the pay per 1K, the minimum payout (that sets the views needed per clip), and the required hashtags, CTA and overlay. Also check for any **"reach X views to share the pool"** rule. On pool campaigns that bar is YOUR OWN total views, so small accounts usually earn nothing from pools. Per-view (CPM) campaigns with a low minimum are where small accounts actually get paid.
